fix(pack): `pack.py` refuses dirty-tree builds and verifies shipped bytes against the stamped rev.

`collect_sources()` ships working-tree bytes while `install.json` stamped
HEAD, with no clean-tree check and no ZIP-vs-blob comparison — the provenance
sidecar could lie about its own archive. The builder now refuses when any
tracked path deviates from HEAD or an include-pattern path is untracked
(escape hatch `HERMES_WF_PACK_ALLOW_DIRTY=1` for non-git dist paths), stamps
the sidecar from one resolved rev, and self-verifies the written ZIP: every
member must match its `SHA256SUMS` row and its git blob at the stamped rev.
A new `--verify` verb re-checks any existing archive and the CI `gates` job
runs pack + verify on every PR, so a release artifact cannot ship with
provenance that failed to check.
