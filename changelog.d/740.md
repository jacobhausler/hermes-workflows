fix(dashboard): co-size the runs-census cache TTL to the measured scan and
the desktop's live refetch cadence (est-fj629, gauntlet-1 line 9).

The shipped pair was intent, not a measured operating point: `RUNS_CACHE_TTL`
(2.0 s) sat BELOW the 4000 ms live refetch, so every active-mode poll
cache-missed and the snapshot collapse — which only ever helps CONCURRENT
pollers — bought nothing at the tuned cadence. The est-ey31s capture behind
it (~1.2 s over ~560 dirs) was an in-file comment with no artifact and had
never been carried to the census bound the desktop defines for itself
(`RUNS_MAX_PAGES` x `RUNS_PAGE_SIZE` = 3000 rows).

This adds the missing measurement as a rerunnable bench
(`scripts/bench_census_latency.py`, source-only) and its committed artifact
(`docs/measurements/census-scan-latency.json`, schema
`wf-census-scan-latency/v1`): linear ~0.5 ms/dir, warm median 1.50 s / max
1.63 s at the 3000-dir bound, 3.36 s at 2x, measured on the loaded serve
host. The TTL co-sizes to poll + measured bound max, rounded up: 6.0 s
(live-mode scan duty <=27% of one worker thread at the bound), and the cache
window now starts at the scan's START, not its end, so scan duration can
never pad the freshness claim. New pins (S1)-(S4) in
`tests/test_runs_offloop_ey31s.py` hold the pair and the start-stamp.
